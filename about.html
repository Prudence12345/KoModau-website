About KoModau Lifestyle Guest House

Where township charm meets understated luxury.

Welcome to KoModau Lifestyle Guest House, where township charm meets understated luxury. Rooted in the vibrant heart of Hammanskraal, KoModau is more than just a destination—it is a celebration of home. Born from the visionary spirit of Mr NS Modau a local resident who grew up in the humble streets of Stinkwater, our guest house stands as a testament to resilience, community pride, and the timeless philosophy that "Local is Lekker." Having walked these paths from childhood, our founder created KoModau to redefine hospitality in the township, proving that world-class luxury belongs right here in our own backyard.

We pride ourselves on offering an exceptional, curated experience that beautifully balances sophisticated comfort with authentic African warmth. Every detail of our establishment—from our meticulously designed rooms and gourmet poolside dining to our tranquil spa experiences—is crafted to offer our guests an upscale sanctuary.

At KoModau, we believe that you do not have to leave the township to experience the finer things in life. We are dedicated to uplifting our community, delivering uncompromising quality, and providing a luxurious escape that makes every guest feel elite, pampered, and right at home.

Experience the soul of Hammanskraal. Experience luxury reimagined.
