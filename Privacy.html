🔒 Privacy Policy

Komodau Guest Lodge | 857 REFENTSE EXT, Hammanskraal | Last Updated: 11 May 2026

1. Introduction

At Komodau Guest Lodge, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

  • Booking Information: Name, surname, phone number, email address, ID/Passport number.
  • Payment Information: EFT proof of payment, card payment reference (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Stay Information: Check-in/check-out dates, room preferences, vehicle registration.
  • Website Usage: IP address, browser type, pages visited via cookies (for analytics).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your booking.
  • To communicate with you via WhatsApp, phone or email (booking confirmations, directions).
  • To improve our services and website experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations (SAPS guest register).
  • We will never sell your personal information.

4. Sharing Your Information

  • We only share information when required by law (e.g., SAPS, SARS).
  • With trusted service providers (payment gateway, website hosting) who are POPIA compliant.
  • We do not share your information with third parties for marketing.

5. Data Security

  • Your information is stored securely and only accessible to authorized staff.
  • EFT and card payments are processed through secure, encrypted channels.
  • We keep booking records for 5 years as required by law, then securely delete them.

6. Cookies

  • Our website uses cookies to remember your preferences and analyze traffic (Google Maps, Analytics).
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work properly.

7. Your Rights (POPIA)

  • Right to Access: You can ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request deletion of your information where legally allowed.
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing for marketing (we don't do marketing spam).
  • To exercise your rights, contact us on 067 707 1884.
